# You Are Angel — Marketing Plan v2 (11 Oct 2026)

Target unchanged from the 11 Oct plan: 10 sales/day by 6 Dec 2026 (week 8) and 50/day by month 5–6. Week 1 = 12–18 Oct 2026.

## Store findings (Shopify read-only, 13 Jul–11 Oct 2026, shop timezone CDT, USD)
- 1,120 sessions; 11 cart-addition sessions; 2 reached checkout; 1 completed. This window differs from the Codex audit (19 Jul–16 Oct: 15 / 7 / 1), so don't compare them directly.
- 847 of 1,120 sessions (76%) landed on `/`.
- 143 active designs. Most have about 3 sets across four sizes (snapshot; stock changes daily).

  - Instagram 3/109

  These are small samples: they show direction, nothing more.
